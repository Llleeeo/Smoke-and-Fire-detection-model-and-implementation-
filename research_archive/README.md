# Fire Factorial Research Archive

This public-safe archive freezes the completed Windows GPU research state as of 2026-08-18.

The formal 3-seed × 4-condition factorial matrix and fixed internal-test evaluation are complete. External pilot results are weak and all external evaluation, training, and new data acquisition have been stopped. No private images, labels, credentials, or checkpoint binaries are included; manifests and SHA-256 hashes provide traceability.

## Reading order

1. `EXPERIMENT_STATUS.md` — completed, failed, blocked, and stopped work.
2. `METRICS_SUMMARY.md` — verified condition statistics, factorial contrasts, gaps, sample sizes, and missingness.
3. `PAPER_RESULTS_DRAFT.md` — cautious manuscript-ready draft.
4. `RUN_REGISTRY.csv`, `INTERNAL_RESULTS.csv`, and `EXTERNAL_RESULTS.csv` — canonical machine-readable results.
5. `CHECKPOINT_MANIFEST.csv` and `DATASET_MANIFEST.csv` — provenance without private artifacts.
6. `LIMITATIONS.md` and `REPRODUCIBILITY.md` — interpretation and rerun constraints.

Figures are derived only from the CSV files and verified summaries. Missing metrics are recorded as `NA`, never as zero.
